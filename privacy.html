Privacy Policy

Tule Savi Design is a web design service operated by Saltonas Bookkeeping LLC. We respect your privacy and collect only the information needed to communicate with you about our services.

Information you send us

When you contact us through an email link on this site, your email provider sends us the information you choose to include, such as your name, email address, business details, and project needs. We use it to respond, discuss services, and prepare quotes if requested. Please do not email passwords, payment-card numbers, or sensitive financial information.

Cookies and analytics

Our website does not currently set marketing cookies or use third-party advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Our hosting provider may process standard technical data, such as IP address and browser information, to deliver and protect the site.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your contact details. We may use email and technical service providers to operate our business. We retain inquiry emails for as long as reasonably necessary to follow up and keep ordinary business records, unless a longer period is required by law.

Your choices

You may request a correction or deletion of contact information by emailing saltonasbookkeeping@gmail.com, subject to any legal recordkeeping obligations.

Updates

We may update this notice if our website or data practices change. The date at the top shows when this notice was last updated.
